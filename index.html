Privacy Policy for MikroBill

Last Updated: June 27, 2026

MikroBill ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how our mobile application handles user information and security standards.

1. Information We Collect and Process

MikroBill is designed as a network administration tool for MikroTik RouterOS. We prioritize user privacy and adhere to strict data minimization principles:

  • Network and Router Credentials: To provide core functionalities—such as managing PPPoE secrets, creating profiles, and monitoring hotspots—the application requires you to input your MikroTik Router IP address (or domain), API port, Username, and Password.
  • Local Storage: All router credentials and configuration data are stored exclusively locally on your device. This data is only used to establish a direct connection to your MikroTik router API.

2. No Third-Party Data Sharing

We do not collect, upload, sync, or store your personal data or router configurations on any external cloud servers or databases managed by us.

  • All network communications occur directly and solely between your mobile device and your designated MikroTik router hardware.
  • We do not sell, trade, rent, or share any information managed through the application with third parties under any circumstances.

3. Android Permissions Required

To function correctly as a native Android network tool, the application requires the following specific permissions:

  • INTERNET: Granted to allow the application to communicate with your MikroTik router API over the local network or internet.
  • ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE: Granted to verify if your device is actively connected to a Wi-Fi or cellular network before initiating router requests.

4. Security

We implement secure coding and storage practices locally within the application package to safeguard your credentials. However, because the application establishes direct external connections to your router, the ultimate security of data in transit depends heavily on your own router network configuration (such as deploying secure VPNs, utilizing custom secure API ports, or enabling HTTPS/SSL where supported by your infrastructure).

5. Children’s Privacy

Our network management tools do not address and are not marketed toward anyone under the age of 13. We do not knowingly or intentionally collect personally identifiable information from children.

6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time to align with app upgrades or platform requirements. You are advised to review this page periodically for any changes. Changes are effective immediately upon being updated here.
